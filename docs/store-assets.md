# Store page

Room Glow Up is a Party & Casual experience. The page should feel like a warm living room, not a countdown. No sale banners, no "last chance", no "limited time", and no claim that stars or votes can be bought.

## Icon

A square cream room seen straight on. A coral armchair sits on a butter rug. A small mint plant and a lavender cushion keep the corner full. Shapes are chunky, outlines are dark ink, and there is no text on the icon.

File: 512×512 PNG.

## Thumbnails

Roblox experience thumbnails are 1920×1080 PNG.

1. **Before and after.** The same small room, plain on the left and dressed for Cocoa Hearth on the right. A butter banner reads the theme name. No timer, no price, no "hurry".
2. **The tour.** Three chunky rooms on a mint stage, with a podium of stars and Auntie Roch at the side. Name plates say Room 1, Room 2, and Room 3. No real usernames.
3. **Building together.** Two players share one wider gingerbread house, trays of candy pieces along the bottom, warm light, plenty of space. The mood is a family night, not a race.

## Description

Party & Casual

Decorate a tiny room, a gingerbread house, or a tree before the clock runs out. Auntie Roch and everyone else tour the rooms and leave stars. Between rounds, grow a dream home, play a shared room with a sibling, and open the shop for style packs and passes.

Holiday themes run through December. After that, the pool switches to winter rooms and trees. A server holds up to 10 players. Solo play still has a critic.

The shop shows crate odds before you buy. Restricted accounts get a fixed set instead of a random crate. Nothing in the shop is a countdown or a sale label. Stars, votes, and a bigger contest room are not for sale.
